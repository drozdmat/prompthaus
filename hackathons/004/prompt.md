# ⚔️ Hackathon #004 – LLM Fight Arena

## Challenge Overview
Forget benchmarks. Forget polite prompts.  
This time, **models fight each other.**

Your goal is to design an **arena** where LLMs can compete, argue, deceive, or outsmart each other.  
Think of it as a social experiment for agents.
A sandbox where logic, persuasion, and chaos collide.

You’re not building *a single model*, you’re building **a world** where they interact.

### 🎯 Core Requirements
- **Multi-Agent Setup**: At least two independent LLMs interacting under defined rules  
- **Game Mechanics**: Scoring, goals, or incentives that shape their behaviour  
- **Arena Design**: The environment, communication protocol, or narrative frame  
- **Transparency**: Provide visibility into *why* each model acts as it does  
- **Replayability**: Every match should be watchable, comparable, or re-run  

### 💡 Feature Ideas
- **Prompt Duel**: Two agents compete to win a judge’s approval  
- **Jailbreak Battle**: One defends the rules, the other tries to break them  
- **Negotiation Game**: Reach an agreement under absurd constraints  
- **Meme War**: Generate the most viral or persuasive message  
- **Adversarial Labyrinth**: Models build traps or challenges for each other  
- **LLM Courtroom**: Prosecution vs Defense (arguments, evidence, chaos)  
- **Commentator Mode**: A third model narrates the fight live  

## 🧩 Bonus Challenges
- Build a **visual or text-based viewer** for spectators
- Introduce **energy limits** (token budgets or cooldowns)
- Allow **spectator voting or audience-triggered events**

## 📅 Time Limit
4 hours
