# 🖼️ Hackathon #001 - Magical Moving Portraits

## Challenge Overview
Create an AI-powered magical portrait system where static images come to life with enchanting animations, subtle movements, and interactive elements. Bring the wonder of animated paintings to the digital realm with modern AI technology.

### 🎯 Core Requirements
- **Portrait Animation**: Transform static images into living, breathing portraits
- **Magical Aesthetics**: Enchanting visual effects, mystical ambiance, and fantasy styling
- **Interactive Elements**: Click, hover, or voice interactions to trigger special animations
- **Smooth Transitions**: Fluid, natural-looking movements that feel organic and magical

### Feature Ideas
- **Image Generation**: Create custom magical portraits from text descriptions or photos
- **Family Portraits**: Create moving portraits of relatives who have passed away, preserving memories and allowing for emotional connections
- **Famous Personalities**: Use public domain image of a famous person and create a moving portrait of them
- **LLM Integration**: Portraits that can speak, tell stories, or respond to questions
- **Voice Synthesis**: Characters with unique voices and personalities
- **Multi-Portrait Gallery**: Multiple animated portraits in a mystical gallery
- **Time-Based Animations**: Portraits that change based on time of day or seasons
- **Story Mode**: Interactive narratives where portraits guide the viewer

## 📅 Time Limit
4 hours
